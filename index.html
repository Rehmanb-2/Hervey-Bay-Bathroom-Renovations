About Bathroom Renovations Hervey Bay

Our Story

Bathroom Renovations Hervey Bay is a locally owned and operated renovation business that has been transforming bathrooms across the Fraser Coast for over 15 years. Founded by local tradesman Mark Thompson, our company was built on the simple principle of delivering quality workmanship with honest, reliable service.

As a licensed and insured renovation specialist, we understand the unique challenges of renovating in our coastal climate. From humidity considerations to salt air protection, we ensure every renovation is built to last in our beautiful Hervey Bay environment.

Our commitment to excellence has earned us a reputation as the Fraser Coast's most trusted bathroom renovation company, with hundreds of satisfied customers and countless referrals from happy homeowners.
